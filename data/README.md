# Extracted research data — not an approved live catalog

**Collected:** 2026-09-27. These files record publicly visible listings and features that were reviewed while planning Quality Glass Emporium. They are **research snapshots**, not current inventory, confirmed prices, verified testimonials, or permission to reproduce third-party artwork.

| File | Contents | How to use |
|---|---|---|
| `shop-profile.json` | Public shop name, contact, address, hours and source/confidence notes. | Confirm the current phone, hours and merchant details with owner before publishing. |
| `existing-site-categories.csv` | Ten categories displayed on the previous Quality Glass website, with links. | Reference for catalog migration; map to the six proposed collections as needed. |
| `existing-site-products.csv` | 32 product cards displayed on the old shop page with slug, text, observed price, regular crossed-out price and source link. | **Do not auto-import as saleable products.** Check actual stock, supplier cost, lawful image rights, final price, dimensions and tax treatment. Photos are **not downloaded or republished**. |
| `competitor-observations.csv` | Observed product-flow/customization/price examples from six competitor sites. | Feature benchmark only; not competitor data to republish. |
| `platform-limits.csv` | Planning limits/constraints and official documentation URLs. | Verify again before depending on a free tier. |
| `feature-requirements.json` | User-requested site structure and operational/payment workflow; separates requirements from verified capabilities. | Backlog input for engineering. |

**Not included:** competitor images, scraped customer reviews, screenshots, protected pages, guessed WhatsApp/UPI credentials, any API tokens, bank details or customer payment evidence. The supplied Google short share link could not be fetched, and a verified merchant UPI ID/QR was not found on the old public site.

The detailed analysis and design remain in [`../research/`](../research/). The product-card export is from the shop owner's *previous public site* and includes old-site marketing claims that require approval; the six competitor observations are deliberately selective rather than an indiscriminate scrape.
