## Proposed live sequence, distinct from the prototype

1. Customer adds one or more products, confirms delivery/pickup and receives a backend-created, unguessable **order ID** and server-calculated **final amount including shipping/tax**. Multi-frame orders first request a shop-approved combined quote; no payment instructions before approved total. Product imagery must be owned/licensed; anime characters and third-party religious artwork require proper rights/permission.
2. Website displays **verified shop UPI QR and UPI ID** only after the owner provides them. Customer pays in UPI app (without assuming an automatic redirect is proof), enters UTR and uploads screenshot to **private object storage** with order-bound authorization and file validation. UTR/screenshot are evidence for **manual review**, not confirmation of money received.
3. Backend stores the pending order and proof (access controlled). Show **Payment pending verification** and a WhatsApp CTA with order reference, item list and UTR. `wa.me` can prefill text but **cannot attach the screenshot automatically**. Either direct the customer to send it as an attachment in WhatsApp, or (preferred) let staff view the proof through the manager panel. Never put a private screenshot URL in the WhatsApp message.
4. A staff member checks the merchant UPI/bank statement for **exact amount, genuine UTR, unique payment, recipient identity and date** before marking paid. Only then reserve/produce the order and notify the customer. Duplicate UTR, mismatched totals, missing receipts and spoofed images require a hold. Track manual verification events and staff action audit.
5. Configure a fallback: customer closes WhatsApp, sends nothing, cancels payment, loses connectivity, uploads wrong screenshot, or sends a WhatsApp message from a different number. The order remains pending and recoverable using the server-side order ID. WhatsApp is for communication, **not the order database**.
